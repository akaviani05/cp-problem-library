Alice and Bob play a game on a tree with $n$ vertices. Alice's piece starts at vertex $x$, and Bob's piece starts at a different vertex $y$. Alice moves first, and then the players take turns.

On a turn, a player must move their own piece along one edge to a vertex that is not occupied by the other piece. A player with no legal move loses. Both players play optimally. Determine the winner.

The original source of this problem is Quera problem 9023: https://quera.org/problemset/9023.
