For any selected edge, both of its endpoints gain one in their degree. Thus
the sum of all requested parities must be even. This condition is also
sufficient on a tree.

Root the tree at vertex $1$. Start with $need[v]=s_v$. Process the vertices in
reverse traversal order, excluding the root. If $need[v]=1$, select the edge
from $v$ to its parent and toggle $need$ at the parent. After processing all
non-root vertices, the root's value is zero exactly when the total parity is
even. If it is zero, the selected edges give every vertex its requested
degree parity.

The algorithm takes $O(n)$ time and $O(n)$ memory. The condition that the
number of $1$s is even can also be checked before constructing the subset.
